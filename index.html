Your first robot, in parts

  • Arduino Uno

    The brain. Students program it from the first session, on their own laptop.

  • Ultrasonic sensor

    The eyes. It measures how far away the wall is, so the robot turns before it hits it.

  • Servo motor

    The neck. It swings the sensor left and right to look for a way through.

  • Motors and driver

    Two geared motors and a driver board turn a line of code into movement.

  • Battery and chassis

    Everything bolts to one plate and runs on two rechargeable cells.

  • All together

    Five parts, one robot that finds its own way round a room. Built and coded by the student in Intermediate.

The Intermediate kit costs ₹2,499, passed on at cost, and it stays with the student. The five lights on the board are the five seats in every batch. The screen on the back is the 16×2 LCD from the Advanced kit.

A weekend in the batch

Two live sessions, Saturday and Sunday. Five cameras on, one kit each, and the founder watching every wire. Nothing is recorded, so when something doesn't work it gets fixed there and then.

Batches are grouped by age and experience, so a 9-year-old and a 19-year-old are never in the same room. Everything runs online, so a student in Pune joins the same batch as one in Patna.
