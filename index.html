Who we are

Small batches, real builds

ShezBotics is live online robotics and AI training, run in batches of five. Students don't watch recordings — they build on their own kit, on camera, with the founder watching the wiring.

Five to a batch

Small enough that every student is asked a question and shows their build every session.

Live, never recorded

Sessions run on Saturday and Sunday. When something doesn't work, it gets debugged there and then.

Kits at cost

Hardware is passed on at what it costs us. No markup, and the components stay with the student.

Anywhere in India

Everything runs online, so a student in any city joins the same batch as everyone else.

Ages 8–20

Batches are grouped by age and experience so the pace suits the room.

Taught by the founder

Every session is run by Shehzad — 120+ projects built, represented India in Dubai.

The founder

Every session is taught by one person

Shehzad Dobariya

Founder · AI & Robotics Analyst

The subject you study doesn't define your future. Your passion does.

I started in the 9th standard, curious about how machines work. I went on to study Commerce, and kept learning AI and robotics on my own — through projects, failed builds and a lot of rewiring.

That took me from local competitions to national platforms, and eventually to representing India at Global Encounters in Dubai.

I teach every ShezBotics batch myself. That is the reason batches are capped at five.
