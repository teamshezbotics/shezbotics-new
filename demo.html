Questions parents ask

Is the demo really free?

Yes. It costs nothing and there is no obligation to enrol afterwards.

Do we need a kit for the demo?

No. Bring nothing but curiosity. The founder builds something small live, and you watch how a batch of five works.

What will the student join on?

Tell us in the form. A laptop or desktop is what most students use; if you're still arranging one, say so and we'll advise.

When do demos run?

On weekends, alongside the live batches. You pick a day and a time window, and we confirm the exact time on WhatsApp.

What happens after the demo?

If it's a fit, we tell you the next batch start date and which level suits. If not, that's completely fine. Courses start at ₹3,499 for Basic.

Can a school book a session instead?

Yes, schools have their own programmes, from two-hour intro sessions to term-long work. See school programmes.
