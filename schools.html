Formats

Three ways to run this

Pick the depth that fits your calendar. All three can run on site or online.

Intro Session

Two hours, up to 30 students. A single hands-on session that introduces robotics and gets every student through one working build. Good for a taster, an activity day, or testing appetite before committing to more.

  • 2 hours · up to 30 students
  • All components brought in and taken away
  • No prior experience or lab needed

from ₹18,000

Workshop Day

A full day, up to 40 students, split into rotating batches so nobody sits watching. Students move through electronics, programming and a final build they present at the end of the day.

  • Full day · up to 40 students
  • Rotating small groups, not one large lecture
  • End-of-day showcase for staff or parents

from ₹45,000

Term Programme

A structured course running across a term, building from electronics through to AI and IoT. Scoped around your timetable, year groups and how many students you want to put through it.

  • Weekly or fortnightly across a term
  • Progress reports and completion certificates
  • Optional competition preparation
