Privacy

How we handle your information

Plain English, no legal padding. If anything here is unclear, email team.shezbotics@gmail.com and we'll explain it.

Last updated 10 August 2026

Who we are

ShezBotics

ShezBotics is a live online AI and robotics academy operated by Shehzad Dobariya, based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. Contact: team.shezbotics@gmail.com, +91 95457 97867.

What we collect

Only what a reply requires

We collect information when you choose to send it through a form on this site:

  • Demo bookings — student name, age, class, school, city, prior experience, parent or guardian name and relationship, phone, email, contact preference, interests, chosen course, learning goals, device and internet access, preferred day and time, intended start, how you heard about us, and any notes you add.
  • Course enquiries — name, student age, phone, email, city, course of interest and your message.
  • School and corporate proposals — organisation name, your name and role, work email, phone, city, group size, format, timeframe and requirements.
  • Nothing automatic — this site sets no advertising or tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics.

Students under 18

Parents submit, always

Most of our students are minors, so this matters more than anything else on this page.

  • Forms concerning a student under 18 should be completed by a parent or guardian. Every form says so, and asks who is submitting.
  • We collect a student's name, age and class only because batches are grouped by age and stage. We do not ask for a minor's address, ID, financial details or any sensitive category of information.
  • We do not create accounts, profiles or logins for students on this website.
  • We never sell, rent or share a student's details for marketing by anyone.
  • A parent or guardian may ask to see, correct or delete their child's information at any time by emailing us, and we will action it within 30 days.
  • If a minor has submitted a form without a parent's involvement, email us and we will delete the record.

Why we hold it

To reply, and to teach

  • To arrange your demo session, answer your enquiry or prepare your proposal.
  • To run a batch you have enrolled in — scheduling, materials and certificates.
  • To keep basic records of the enquiries and bookings we have received.
  • We do not send marketing emails or newsletters. We contact you about the thing you contacted us about.

Where it goes

Two places, both ours

Form submissions are recorded in a private Google Sheet in our own Google Workspace account and emailed to team.shezbotics@gmail.com. Access is restricted to the founder. Classes run on standard video conferencing software. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security terms; we do not pass your information to anyone else, and we never sell it.

How long

Enquiries: 24 months

Enquiries and demo bookings that do not lead to enrolment are deleted within 24 months. Records for enrolled students are kept while they are studying with us and for three years afterwards, so we can reissue certificates. You can ask us to delete anything sooner and we will, unless we are legally required to keep it.

Your rights

Ask, and we act

You can ask us what we hold about you or your child, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, or withdraw consent for us to contact you. Email team.shezbotics@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days, and we will not ask you to justify the request.

Photographs

Consent first

Where a student's photograph or work appears on this site or our social media, we obtain permission from a parent or guardian beforehand. If you would like an image of your child removed, email us and we will take it down promptly.

Changes

Dated at the top

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. For anything you would like clarified, write to team.shezbotics@gmail.com and you will get a real answer.
